# Boss Bucks interior page design

Status: proposed desktop mockup, awaiting owner design approval.
Reference: `boss-bucks-desktop-proposed.png`.

The owner approved the full homepage and directed continuation with interior
pages. This is the first major interior-page design, following section 91 of
his master website handoff: mockup, approval, implementation, deployment,
browser comparison, corrections and review. The live homepage remains intact.

## Design system

Follow the approved homepage black/orange/white theme, header, B+ identity,
heavy Inter typography, rectangular orange actions and photographic realism.
Keep the exact approved discounts interface as the product visual. Artwork
is illustrative and must not imply actual customers or merchant partnerships.

## Page story

1. Save Like a Boss: useful discounts and fundraising toward family costs.
2. Explain the two distinct benefits: discount membership and earned Boss Bucks.
3. Discount product showcase: nearby deals, favorites, offer/redemption rules,
   and configurable geographic coverage. Coverage varies by membership.
4. Help cover approved costs: sports fees, registration, team gear, children’s
   and youth camps, tournaments and eligible travel. The family cost illustration
   shows $450 registration, $300 applied value and $150 remaining, clearly labeled
   as an illustrative example rather than a fundraising outcome or entitlement.
5. Coming soon: use Boss Bucks to purchase gas and restaurant gift cards.
   Approved spending rules apply.
6. Fundraise Like a Boss: join an approved campaign, raise value and apply it to
   approved costs. Show cards, offered trial invitations, donations and Money Board.
7. Integrity: family/participant value cannot cash out or transfer to personal bank
   accounts. Only eligible organization proceeds may transfer to an approved
   organization bank account. Family and organizational balances are distinct.
8. Final fundraising CTA and the approved website footer.

## Implementation details after approval

- All marketing copy and interactive controls must be semantic HTML, not a page-sized
  raster. Preserve the mockup's composition, typography, spacing and photographic feel.
- Use the exact approved header mark and existing approved discounts source artwork.
- Label product/expense examples illustrative; label wallet capability platform vision.
- Do not represent unlaunched functionality as a working checkout or wallet.
- In the product benefit copy, use "participating businesses" rather than the generated
  image's "local and national merchants" wording; geographic access is configurable.
- Do not invent pricing, trial entitlement, allocation rules or donation thresholds.
- Detailed 30/60/90-day trial choices and campaign-specific thank-you gift conditions
  belong on the separately reviewed Fundraising page.
- Preserve the existing lead forms and routes. No platform/backend/payment changes.
- Adapt the desktop design to phones, then implement and compare actual browser renders.
- Review preview only. Production cutover remains a separate owner decision.

The proposed raster was created with the built-in Imagegen tool, using the approved
homepage and approved Boss Bucks Discounts screen as references. No new live page
was published in this design checkpoint.
