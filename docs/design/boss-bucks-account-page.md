## One account that keeps up with your family

A basketball season. A church youth camp. A club tournament. Another child's school or community activity. Family life rarely fits inside a single organization.

Boss is designed around that reality. Parents can connect multiple children and participating organizations in one family account. Each child's personal fundraising link and QR code identify the participant and campaign they support. Parents can see progress by child, team and organization, alongside a combined family view of eligible credits.

One place to manage your activity does not erase the rules attached to funds. Credits remain associated with their source and applicable organization or campaign restrictions. A combined view does not mean every balance can be spent on every activity.

## How fundraising becomes opportunity

1. Connect with a participating organization. Join the approved team, church, ministry, school or community campaign associated with your child.
2. Share the right fundraiser. Use the participant's assigned link or QR code so eligible support is attributed to the correct child and campaign.
3. Follow your progress. See fundraising activity and the eligible credits allocated under that campaign's rules. The amount contributed by a supporter is not automatically the amount available to a family.
4. Put available credits to work. Apply eligible value toward approved activity costs through the program's authorized spending process.

Campaigns can include physical or digital Boss Bucks discount cards, donations and the Digital Money Board. Offered features and allocation rules vary by campaign. An organization's fundraising terms determine the share supporting its program and any eligible participant or family credit. Do not assume an automatic split or that the full purchase or donation becomes family credit.

## Two benefits. Clearly explained.

### Boss Bucks Discounts

A discount membership gives supporters access to participating business offers under the membership's coverage and offer rules. It helps supporters save while supporting an approved fundraiser.

### Earned Boss Bucks

Eligible fundraising credits help families cover approved participation expenses. This is restricted spending value governed by program rules, separate from access to merchant discounts.

Buying a membership does not mean the purchase price becomes a personal spending balance. A merchant discount also does not automatically add money to a family's fundraising account.

## Help cover the cost of showing up

Eligible uses depend on the organization, campaign and available program features.

- Fees and registration: approved sports, team and organization participation costs.
- Gear and equipment: approved items needed for the child's participating activity, including required uniforms where authorized.
- Camps and events: approved sports camps, children's camps, church youth camps and organization events.
- Tournaments: approved tournament and related participation expenses.
- Eligible travel and lodging: approved transportation and hotel costs connected to team or organization activities, subject to the available spending process.

The purpose is to help families cover eligible costs connected to participation. Ordinary household purchases, unrelated trips and unrestricted personal spending fall outside that purpose. Parents should confirm eligibility before incurring an expense; an out-of-pocket purchase does not automatically qualify for repayment.

### Coming soon: more ways to cover the journey

Planned fuel and restaurant gift cards would help cover approved activity-related travel and meal expenses. These benefits are intended for eligible team and organization expenses and remain subject to program rules. Gift-card redemption is not presented as available today. Vendors, limits and redemption procedures will be provided as benefits become available.

## Built around purpose and accountability

The Boss model keeps family credits and organizational proceeds distinct. Families use eligible credits for approved costs. Only eligible organizational proceeds may settle to an approved organization bank account.

Family Boss Bucks cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to a personal bank account. The account is designed to support participation through approved spending, rather than unrestricted personal transfers.

The intended account experience keeps the source, allocation and use of credits identifiable. Organization and campaign rules govern eligibility. Families need a clear view of what is available to use and what remains subject to processing or review. Spending records should make it possible to understand which approved expense used a credit.

These are the operating principles for the planned account experience. The final approval steps, receipt requirements, handling of refunds and adjustments, and redemption methods must match the verified platform before being advertised as working features.

## Common questions

Can one account include more than one child?
Yes. The planned family model supports multiple children, each with participant attribution and a combined parent view.

Can we participate through multiple organizations?
Yes. Participating teams, churches, schools and community organizations can appear in the same family view. Each campaign's rules still apply.

Can I move funds freely between children or organizations?
A shared view is not permission to reassign restricted funds. Transfers or cross-activity use must follow the applicable program and organization rules. No unrestricted transfer feature is promised.

Can I withdraw the balance or send it to my bank?
No. Family credits are for approved expenses. Only eligible organizational proceeds may settle to an approved organization bank account.

Can Boss Bucks reimburse any expense I have already paid?
An expense must be eligible and follow an authorized process. This page does not promise personal cash reimbursement. Confirm eligibility and the available payment method before spending.

Are hotel costs and gift cards available now?
Eligible activity-related travel and lodging are part of the intended expense scope, subject to available redemption methods. Fuel and restaurant gift cards are coming soon. Do not assume these features are already operational.

Does fundraising guarantee every expense will be covered?
No. Available support depends on fundraising results, campaign allocations and eligibility. The goal is to expand opportunity and reduce financial pressure.
