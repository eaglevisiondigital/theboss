# Phase 8B boundary

Phase 8A implements native Boss offers only. No partner feed, external catalog,
licensed content import, SSO or travel booking is operational. Future adapters
must preserve provider/source provenance and explicitly validate licensing,
territory, product, entitlement mapping, attribution, sharing terms and mode.
Integration activation requires separate Phase 8B approval. No unlicensed
scraping, Shopify or GoAffPro dependency is allowed.

Future combined discovery must retain native-versus-partner provenance. Travel
availability, discount and commissionability are separate facts; no booking or
gift-card marketplace is implemented here. Future sponsored placement must stay
separate from organic relevance; promotional providers and billing remain off.
