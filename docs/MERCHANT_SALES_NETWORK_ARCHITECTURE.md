# Merchant sales network, Phase 8A

Implementation checkpoint; live release remains outstanding.

Boss owns leads, opportunities, activities and acquisition attribution. Reps
reuse canonical people. Explicit sales assignments bind approved markets and
optional manager oversight; no home-address inference or national feed. Reps
see their own assigned opportunities. Managers see only assigned territory/reps.
Neither relationship grants merchant employee or consumer membership authority.

Finite pipeline states and bounded call/email/meeting/follow-up/note/onboarding
activities preserve assignment and conversion history. Conversion creates one
prospect merchant and preserves source attribution; it does not approve the
merchant, invent an owner, or create billing. Reassignment and conversion share
transactional fences. No Salesforce, external sending, commissions calculation
or commission payment engine is introduced. Attribution is separate from Wallet,
fundraising, membership and discount redemption.
