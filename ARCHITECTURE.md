# Architecture

## Frontend
Next.js App Router + TypeScript + CSS.

## Hosting
Netlify preview first. Production domain is not to be pointed at this build until approval.

## Future backend
Supabase-compatible lead capture and CRM architecture. Lead types must remain distinguishable:
- Fundraising Organization
- Merchant Partner
- General Inquiry
- Sales Rep Interest
- Support

## Attribution
Forms should preserve page source, UTM source, timestamp and form type.

## Product architecture
The public site is a presentation layer for one ecosystem. Product experiences may later become authenticated applications without forcing a marketing-site rewrite.
