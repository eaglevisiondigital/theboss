# Decisions

1. Repository: `eaglevisiondigital/theboss`.
2. Master brand: BOSS PLUS.
3. The new site replaces the historical site. It does not recreate it.
4. Digital Boss Bucks is the future-facing flagship discount experience.
5. Physical saver cards are optional/transitional and are not central to the initial website build.
6. Boss Fundraising is universal across qualifying teams and organizations.
7. Boss Money Board receives a major standalone product page.
8. Boss Engage and Family Hub are major product areas.
9. Kingdom Raise is not a major public Boss sub-brand on this site.
10. Preview/staging must be approved before production domain cutover.
